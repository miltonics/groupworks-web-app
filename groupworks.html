About this app

A pocket companion to the Group Works card deck — 91 patterns of healthy group process, created by the Group Pattern Language Project and shared under a Creative Commons BY‑SA license. Browse the patterns, draw one at random for guidance, or lay a spread to design or debrief a gathering.

The short text on each card here is an original plain-language summary written for this app — not the official card wording. Every card links to its full official pattern at groupworksdeck.org.

Adding the card artwork

To show the real card faces (which carry the official text), create a cards/ folder next to this file and add a JPG per pattern, named with lowercase words joined by underscores:

  • cards/aesthetics_of_space.jpg
  • cards/yes_and.jpg
  • cards/seeing_the_forest_seeing_the_trees.jpg

When an image exists it replaces the text face automatically; any card without one keeps its summary face. The free deck download at groupworksdeck.org has the artwork.
